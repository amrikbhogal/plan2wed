Your day, thoughtfully planned

Make room for the moments that matter.

Thoughtfully created for Sikh weddings, Plan2Wed brings together the celebrations, family, and details surrounding your Anand Karaj. From the joy of your Jaggo and the colour of your Mehndi to the ceremony at the Gurdwara and the meal that follows, make space for the traditions and moments that matter to you.

Whether you’re planning as a couple or with the support of family and friends, keep your events, venues, guest lists, seating arrangements, and shopping in one calm, considered place. Follow your budget as plans take shape, bring each celebration together at your own pace, and spend more time looking forward to sharing it all with the people you love.

Beginning with Sikh weddings, we’re building towards a home for other religious wedding traditions too—with space for the customs and moments that matter to you.
